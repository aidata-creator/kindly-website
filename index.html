The shift

AI broke the old outsourcing model.

01

The seat-seller's dilemma

Traditional BPOs bill by the seat. Every workflow they automate is revenue they lose — so they don't. You keep paying for headcount that software should have replaced years ago.

02

The automation trap

Pure-AI vendors automate everything, then quality collapses on the work that needs judgment — edge cases, escalations, regulated reviews, brand voice. The errors reach your customers.

You shouldn't have to choose between yesterday's headcount and tomorrow's error rate.

We're paid either way. That changes everything.

Seat-billers profit when nothing changes. AI vendors profit when everything does. We assess every workflow on its merits and tell you the truth about it — because our business works whether the answer is software or people. Your operating cost goes down either way.

Before you ask

The questions that decide it.

How do you guarantee quality with a distributed workforce?

Every unit passes multi-layer QA: gold-standard calibration tasks, double-blind sampling, and dedicated QA analysts at roughly one per fifteen specialists. You see accuracy rates on a weekly dashboard, and our SLAs carry credits. With per-output pricing, defective units are simply never billed.

How do you handle data security and privacy?

Client data is accessed through controlled environments — role-based access, NDA-bound specialists, and for sensitive engagements, virtual desktop setups where data never leaves your infrastructure. We work under your data processing agreement and are building toward ISO 27001. For regulated work we scope controls with your compliance team before launch. Full security details →

What about time zones?

Output-based work is asynchronous by design: you send volume, we return QA'd results against a turnaround SLA. For real-time coverage we staff to your business hours — the Philippines overlaps naturally with Australia and Asia, and we run dedicated shifts for US and European hours.

Why the Philippines?

The world's most experienced outsourcing workforce, with exceptional English fluency, deep familiarity with Western business culture, and among the highest AI-tool adoption rates of any workforce globally. The talent was never in question. The model was — and ours pays that talent fairly and equips it with modern tools.

Isn't "fairly paid" going to make you more expensive?

Our all-in rates are competitive with any quality Philippine provider and well below onshore equivalents. Fair pay reduces the costs that actually hurt: attrition, retraining, and error rates. You get a stable team that keeps your context instead of a revolving door — and because we automate aggressively, you're paying for judgment, not keystrokes.

What if the audit says we should just automate everything?

Then that's what the report will say, with the implementation plan to do it — with us or without us. The fee is fixed and credited if you engage us for delivery. We'd rather be trusted than hired.

How fast can we actually start?

A free 100-unit pilot can begin within days of scoping. A full team is recruited, trained on your guidelines, calibrated through QA, and live in two to three weeks. The audit runs two to three weeks end to end.
